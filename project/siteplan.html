Site Name

Nigerian Art Hub

This name was chosen because it represents a central place where visitors can discover Nigerian artists, explore artwork, and learn about the rich cultural heritage of Nigeria.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to showcase Nigerian artwork and artists, promote cultural heritage, and provide information about exhibitions, art collections, and cultural events throughout Nigeria.

Scenarios

  • Which Nigerian artists and artworks are featured on the website?
  • Where can I find information about upcoming art exhibitions and events?
  • How can I learn more about a specific artist and their work?

Color Schema

Deep Blue (#2D2D72) – Used for headers, navigation, section headings, and footer.

Light Gray (#F4F4F4) – Used for page background.

Gold (#D4A017) – Used for buttons, highlights, and accent elements.

Typography

Georgia

Used for headings and section titles.

Arial

Used for body text, navigation, and general content.
